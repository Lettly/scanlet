Scanlet bridge

Lets Scanlet on this computer reach eSCL / AirScan printers directly — no local server, no drivers. Whenever you are not using it, the extension sits idle.

Network access

Checking…

To talk to a printer, the extension needs permission to reach addresses on your local network. Without it, nothing is probed and nothing is sent anywhere.

Where to look

Printers are found by asking addresses on your network. List the /24 prefixes to sweep, or leave it empty to use the two most common home networks.

    Then

    Open Scanlet, choose Scanner → Browser extension, and press Scan. If a printer is missed, add it by address there — the extension will ask for permission for that one device only.

    This extension reaches eSCL / AirScan printers only. A scanner that has just a Linux driver — a Samsung M2070, say — is driven by the Scanlet bridge with SANE instead.
