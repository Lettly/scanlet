Share target harness

Android's share sheet sends a POST of multipart form data to the manifest's share_target.action, which only a service worker can receive. This page submits the same form, so the whole path — the worker stashes the files, redirects, the app imports them — can be exercised without a phone.

  1. Open Scanlet once on this origin, so the service worker installs.
  2. Come back here and press the button.
  3. The browser lands on Scanlet with the two pages already imported.

Expected: a navigation to index.html?shared=<id>, and a document holding two pages, one dark and one light.
